Recurring issues and problem candidates

Review trigger: 3 or more incidents with the same symptom within 7 days. Problem candidate: 5 or more in 7 days at 3x the normal rate, which suggests a common root cause

Insights and recommended actions

    Resolver group performance

    Resolution SLA
    Resolved within the priority target (P1 4 h, P2 8 h, P3 24 h, P4 72 h, P5 120 h), calendar hours, no pause states.
    Response SLA
    Acknowledged within P1 15 min, P2 30 min, P3 2 h, P4 8 h, P5 24 h.
    First-contact resolution
    Incident resolved by the Service Desk on the first contact without reassignment.
    Problem candidate
    Same symptom 5+ times within 7 days and at least 3x its normal weekly rate. Any symptom seen 3+ times in 7 days meets the review trigger.
    Aged backlog
    Open tickets older than 14 days, as of the end date (a snapshot that does not change with the range).
